Measure Neck Circumference

Instructions:

  • Stand upright with your head facing forward.
  • Place the tape measure just below the **larynx (Adam's apple)**, ensuring the tape measure is **level** and not slanted up or down.
  • Do not pull the tape tight; allow it to rest gently on the skin.
  • Take the measurement on a **normal exhale**.

Measure at the smallest circumference point, just below the larynx.

​

Measure Waist Circumference

Instructions:

  • For **men and women**, measure horizontally around the abdomen at the level of the **navel (belly button)**.
  • Ensure the tape measure is **level** all the way around the body.
  • Take the measurement on a **normal exhale**.

Measure horizontally at the navel level (belly button).

​

Measure Hip Circumference (Female Only)

Instructions:

  • This measurement is **only required for females**.
  • Measure horizontally around the **widest part of the hips/buttocks**.
  • Ensure the tape measure is level.

Measure horizontally at the widest part of the hips/buttocks.
